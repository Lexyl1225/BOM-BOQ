1Introduction

BOM / BOQ Studio Pro is the company's tool for preparing engineering cost documents and moving them through review, pricing, approval and purchasing, all in one place, with every step recorded.

  • A Bill of Materials (BOM) lists the materials a project needs: description, unit, quantity, unit cost and total.
  • A Bill of Quantities (BOQ) is similar, but splits each line into materials and labor costs.
  • A Cost Estimate combines a document's material total with labor, allowances and other costs into one project figure.
  • MCE (Materials Cost Estimate) is the Purchasing step where verified supplier prices are entered.

How a document moves through the system

Create & saveAn engineer builds a BOM or BOQ and saves it.Status: Saved
Send to MCEThe document is sent to Purchasing for pricing.Status: Pending price edit
Price & postPurchasing enters unit costs and posts it to a department.Status: Posted
ApproveThe department's admin reviews and approves it.Status: Approved
Purchase orderPurchasing attaches quotations and PO files.Approved for PO

Signing in

  1. Open the application. You are taken to the Sign In screen.
  2. Enter your username and password, then click Sign In.
  3. If this is your first sign-in (or an Administrator reset your password), you are asked to set a new password before you can continue. See Change your password.

Creating an account

  1. On the sign-in screen, switch to Create Account.
  2. Choose a username and password, select your Account Role and Department.
  3. Submit the form, then sign in with your new account.

Administrator accounts can only be created with an existing Administrator's credentials. If you are unsure which role to choose, pick User (Department Member) and ask an Administrator to adjust it.

Finding your way around

Sidebar (left)

  • Pages are grouped into Overview, Documents, Workflow, Administration and Support.
  • The highlighted item is the page you are on.

3Pages & Features

A tour of every page, what it is for, and what each button does.

Dashboard Everyone

Your starting point after signing in.

  • Quick buttons: New BOM, New BOQ and New Estimate.
  • Summary cards: total documents, pending price edits, documents posted for approval, approved for PO, cost estimates and active users.
  • Quick Actions & Workflows: shortcut tiles to each page you can use, plus links to the Accomplishment Report and the DAC Department Portal.
  • Recent System Activity: the latest actions in the system. Administrators can click View Full Log.

Create BOM Users, Dept. Admins, Admins

An interactive Bill of Materials form with live totals.

Project information

  • Department, Project Name, Location, Total Floor Area and Date.
  • Reference no. is generated automatically (for example MCE-20261125143005) and cannot be edited.

Sections and rows

  • The form starts with standard sections: Panel Board & Circuit Breakers, Wires & Cables, Lighting Fixtures, Auxiliary Lighting, Convenience Outlets & Switches, Conduit & Fittings, and Miscellaneous.
  • Each row has Item Description, Unit, Qty and Unit Cost. Total Cost = Qty × Unit Cost and updates as you type.
  • Under every section: + Add Row, − Remove Row (removes the last row), Delete Section, and + Add Section (pick a section type from the list).
  • The Grand Total and the signature blocks (Prepared by, Checked by, Approved by…) are at the bottom.

Toolbar buttons

  • Print: prints the form.
  • Clear Form: resets the form to start over.
  • Create BOQ: switches to the BOQ builder.
  • Save: asks for a document name and saves it.
  • View Save Files / View MCE: jump to Saved Documents or MCE Pricing.
{{ icon('edit', 18) }}

Edit mode: when you open a saved document with Edit, a blue EDIT MODE banner appears at the top. Use Update Document to save your changes to the same document, or Cancel to go back without saving. The normal Save button is hidden so you don't create a duplicate by accident.

Create BOQ Users, Dept. Admins, Admins

Works exactly like the BOM builder, with the same project fields, sections and buttons, but each row has separate cost columns:

  • Materials: Unit Cost and Total Cost.
  • Labor: Unit Cost and Total Cost.
  • Total Cost = materials total + labor total for the row, and the Grand Total adds up every row.

Saved Documents Everyone

The library of every BOM and BOQ you are allowed to see.

  • Summary cards and tabs filter the list: All, BOM, BOQ, Unposted for Correction, Posted, Approved for PO.
  • Search by name, reference no., project, department or creator, and filter by department. Use Clear filters to reset.
  • Row actions:
    • Edit: opens the document in its builder (Edit mode).
    • Preview: opens a clean, printable view in a new tab.
    • Print: opens the print dialog straight away.
    • MCE: sends the document to Purchasing for pricing.
    • Delete: permanently deletes the document (you are asked to confirm).
  • A lock icon marks approved documents. Their Edit, MCE and Delete buttons are disabled for everyone except Administrators.

Cost Estimates Users, Dept. Admins, Admins

Builds a full project cost from a priced document plus other costs.

  • Source Documents: lists Posted documents (read-only). Choose one in the drop-down, and its total becomes the Total Materials Cost Estimate.
  • Cost Breakdown: enter the Estimate / Project Name, then Labor, Meal Allowance, Transportation Allowance and Mobilization / Demobilization.
  • + Add Item adds your own lines (for example permits or equipment rental).
  • The Grand Total Cost Estimate (in ₱) updates automatically.
  • Save Estimate stores it. Recent Estimates lets you View, Print, Edit or Delete saved estimates.
  • Print / Export produces a printable report (choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog for a PDF).

MCE Pricing Purchasing & Admins

Where Purchasing enters verified supplier prices and sends documents on for approval.

  • Summary cards show pending and posted counts. Search filters every table.
  • Pending Price Edit: documents waiting for pricing.
    • View: open a read-only copy.
    • Edit Unit Cost: opens the document with the cost fields highlighted (blue = BOM unit cost, dark blue = BOQ material cost, orange = BOQ labor cost). Totals recalculate as you type. Click Save Changes.
    • Post: choose the target department and confirm. The document moves to Posted for PO and appears in that department's Approvals.
  • Posted for PO: documents already posted, showing their target department. Actions: View, Print, Unpost (moves it back to Pending for correction) and, for Administrators only, Remove.
  • Rename: click the pencil icon beside any document name, type the new name and press Enter (or Esc to cancel). Names must be unique.
  • Administrators also see the saved BOM and BOQ lists on this page.
{{ icon('trash', 18) }}

Remove deletes the document permanently, not just from this list, so it is available to Administrators only. To send a document back for changes, use Unpost instead.

Approvals Department Admins & Admins

  • Lists documents Posted to your department (Administrators see every department).
  • Cards at the top show pending, approved and total counts. Use Refresh to reload.
  • View opens the document in a preview window, which you can Print.
  • Approve asks for confirmation, then locks the document and sends it to Purchase Orders.

Purchase Orders Purchasing & Admins

  • Lists every Approved document with who approved it and when.
  • View / Print: open or print the final document.
  • Attach File: upload quotations, drawings or purchase requisitions. Click or drag files onto the upload area. Accepted: PDF, JPG, PNG, up to 10 MB each.
  • Each attachment can be previewed, downloaded, opened in a new tab, or deleted.

Administration Administrators

Users

  • + Add New User: username, role, department, optional email and an initial password. New users must change it at first sign-in.
  • Edit: change role, department or account status (Active / Suspended).
  • Password: Reset password creates a temporary password, shown once, so copy it. Change password sets a specific one.
  • Delete removes an account. Sync to JSON Backup writes a backup copy of all users.

API Keys

  • Every department has its own API key. Browser users don't need to do anything, because the app sends the key automatically.
  • You can Generate, Regenerate, Deactivate / Activate, Revoke or reveal and Copy a key, and see when it was last used.
{{ icon('key', 18) }}

Deactivating or revoking a department's key stops everyone in that department from loading or saving data until it is reactivated or regenerated.

Audit Logs

  • A complete record of sign-ins, saves, edits, postings, approvals, uploads and admin actions.
  • Filter by search text, user, action and date, then Apply. Use Previous / Next to page through.
  • Export CSV or Export JSON to download the results.

Your account & themes Everyone

  • Change password: account menu (top right) → Change password.
  • Themes: click the palette icon. Choose from Default, Dark, Light, Corporate, Glassmorphism, Cyberpunk, Sci-Fi, Neon and Anime. Your choice is remembered on this browser.
  • Sign out: account menu → Sign out, or the Logout button on pages that have one.

4Step-by-Step Guides

Click a task to expand it.

Create and save a new BOM or BOQ Users
  1. In the sidebar, click Documents → Create BOM (or Create BOQ).
  2. Fill in the project information: Department, Project Name, Location, Floor Area and Date.
  3. For each section, type the item descriptions, units and quantities. Enter unit costs if you know them (Purchasing can set them later).
  4. Use + Add Row for more lines, and + Add Section for another category. Remove what you don't need with Delete Section.
  5. Check the Grand Total and fill in the signature names.
  6. Click Save, type a clear document name and confirm.
  7. The document now appears in Saved Documents with status Saved.
Edit a saved document Users
  1. Open Saved Documents and find the document (use search or the tabs).
  2. Click Edit. The builder opens with the blue EDIT MODE banner.
  3. Make your changes, then click Update Document in the banner.
  4. To leave without saving, click Cancel.

Approved documents can only be edited by an Administrator.

Send a document to Purchasing for pricing Users
  1. In Saved Documents, find the finished document.
  2. Click MCE and confirm.
  3. The status changes to Pending price edit, and Purchasing sees it in MCE Pricing.
Price a document and post it to a department Purchasing
  1. Open Workflow → MCE Pricing.
  2. In Pending Price Edit, click Edit Unit Cost on the document.
  3. Enter the verified supplier prices in the highlighted fields. Row totals and the grand total update automatically.
  4. Click Save Changes.
  5. Click Post, choose the Target department, and click Post to Department.
  6. The document moves to Posted for PO and appears in that department's Approvals.
Rename a document Purchasing & Admins
  1. On MCE Pricing, hover over the document and click the pencil icon next to its name.
  2. Type the new name and press Enter or click Save.
  3. If the name is already used by another document you'll see a message. Choose a different name.
Send a posted document back for correction Purchasing & Admins
  1. On MCE Pricing, find the document under Posted for PO.
  2. Click Unpost and confirm.
  3. The document returns to Pending Price Edit with status Unposted. Re-price it and post again.
  4. The owning department also sees it under Saved Documents → Unposted for Correction.
Review and approve a document Department Admins
  1. Open Workflow → Approvals.
  2. Click View to check the line items, quantities and prices.
  3. If everything is correct, click Approve and confirm.
  4. The document becomes Approved, is locked, and goes to Purchase Orders.

If something is wrong, don't approve it. Ask Purchasing to Unpost it for correction.

Attach purchase order files Purchasing
  1. Open Workflow → Purchase Orders.
  2. Find the approved document and click Attach File (or + Attach More).
  3. Drag your files onto the upload area, or click it to browse. Only PDF, JPG and PNG up to 10 MB are accepted.
  4. The files appear under the document, where you can preview, download or delete them.
Build a project cost estimate Users
  1. Open Documents → Cost Estimates.
  2. Under Source Documents, choose a posted document from the drop-down. Its total fills in the materials cost.
  3. Enter an Estimate / Project Name.
  4. Fill in Labor, Meal and Transportation allowances, and Mobilization / Demobilization.
  5. Add any extra costs with + Add Item.
  6. Check the Grand Total, then click Save Estimate.
  7. Use Print / Export for a printable report.
Print a document or save it as PDF Everyone
  1. From Saved Documents, click the Print icon (or Preview, then Print).
  2. In your browser's print dialog, pick a printer, or choose Save as PDF as the destination.
  3. For best results use A4 or Letter, and enable Background graphics so the coloured headers print.
Change your password Everyone
  1. Click your name in the top-right corner and choose Change password.
  2. Enter your current password, then the new password twice.
  3. Passwords need at least 8 characters, with at least one letter and one number, and must differ from your username.

Changing your password signs you out of your other sessions.

{% if _is_admin %}
Add a user or reset a password Administrators
  1. Open Administration → Users.
  2. To add someone, click + Add New User, fill in username, role, department and an initial password, then click Create User.
  3. To reset a password, click Password on the user's row → Reset password, then Copy the temporary password and give it to the user securely.
  4. The user must choose a new password at their next sign-in.
{% endif %}

5Tips & Best Practices

Naming documents

  • Use a consistent pattern, e.g. Project – Area – BOM.
  • Include the building or floor so documents are easy to find later.
  • Names must be unique. Rename on MCE Pricing if needed.

Accurate totals

  • Enter quantities as numbers only; put the unit (pcs, m, lot) in the Unit column.
  • Delete empty rows and unused sections before saving.
  • Double-check the Grand Total before sending to MCE.

Working efficiently

  • Use search and the summary cards to jump to the documents you need.
  • Use Preview to check a document without risking changes.
  • Press Refresh on workflow pages to see the latest updates from other users.

Staying safe

  • Never share your password. Each action is logged under your name.
  • Sign out on shared computers.
  • Approve only after viewing the full document; approval locks it.

6Troubleshooting

I can't see a page in the sidebar

Pages are shown according to your role and department (see Roles & permissions). If you need access, ask an Administrator to update your account. Opening a restricted page directly sends you back to the Dashboard.

The Edit, MCE or Delete button is greyed out
  • The document is Approved and locked. Only an Administrator can change it.
  • Or you are in the Purchasing department, which has view-only access to saved documents.
  • Or you are signed in as a Guest (read-only).

Hover over the button to see the exact reason.

I'm asked to change my password every time I sign in

Your new password probably didn't meet the rules: at least 8 characters, one letter and one number, and different from your username. Make sure both password fields match, then submit again.

I don't see my document in Saved Documents
  • Click Clear filters; a tab, search or department filter may be hiding it.
  • Click Refresh to reload the list.
  • You only see documents from your own department (Administrators see everything).
Preview or Print does nothing

Your browser is probably blocking pop-ups. Allow pop-ups for this site (look for the blocked pop-up icon in the address bar), then try again.

My file upload was rejected

Only PDF, JPG and PNG files up to 10 MB are accepted, and the file content must match its extension. Convert other formats to PDF, or compress large scans, and try again.

"A document named … already exists"

Document names must be unique. Add a project, area or date to make the name different.

Lists won't load, or I see "API key" or "401/403" errors
  1. Refresh the page. If that fails, sign out and sign in again.
  2. If the whole department is affected, the department's API key may have been deactivated. Contact an Administrator, who can reactivate it on the API Keys page.
I was signed out unexpectedly

Sessions expire after a period of time, and changing or resetting your password signs out your other sessions. Simply sign in again; saved work is not lost.
